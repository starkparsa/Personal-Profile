Follow-on to the InverTrac pupil-detection GUI project, adapting the same low-overhead
eye-tracking algorithm into a WebAPI for automated student attendance and registration.

- Led a team of 4 extending InverTrac into a web-accessible service, keeping the same
  webcam-only, low CPU/GPU footprint.
- Built a user-centric platform in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Python, improving user
  satisfaction by 50%.
- Published as *"Student Attendance and Registration Using InverTrac as WebAPI"* in the
  *International Journal of Early Childhood Special Education* (Oct 2022).
