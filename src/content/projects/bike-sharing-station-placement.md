Urban-mobility ML project forecasting bike-share demand zones to guide station placement decisions.

- Combined an RNN (temporal demand patterns) with a CNN (spatial/geographic relationships) into a single hybrid model.
- Integrated transit ridership, weather, and traffic data to correlate subway usage with bike-sharing demand.
- Produced interactive maps (Folium) to support municipal planning decisions, with reinforcement learning for adaptive optimization flagged as future work.
